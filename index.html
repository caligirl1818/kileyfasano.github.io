About

Background

I'm Kiley Fasano — a designer, production lead, and consultant based in California with over fifteen years of experience spanning graphic design, web, interactive media, and large-scale digital production.

My career took shape at the intersection of design craft and operational rigor. After studying Web Design and Interactive Media at the Art Institute of Colorado, I spent years in graphic design and web design before moving into digital production — building the systems and workflows that make high-volume creative output possible.

I spent over a decade at Apple as a Graphic Production Lead, working across interactive product launch campaigns for apple.com. Half of that as a contractor, half as full-time — giving me perspective on both sides of how creative organizations work. My focus was high-volume, multi-market localization: coordinating complex production pipelines that had to ship flawlessly, simultaneously, across the globe.

Today I work with companies that need both design sensibility and operational discipline — whether that means helping a new business get off the ground, shaping a brand identity, or bringing production precision to a digital team that's outgrown its processes.
